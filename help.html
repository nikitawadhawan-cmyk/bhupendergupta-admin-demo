How to write a story

You can print this page and keep it next to the computer.

  1. Start a new story

    On the My stories page, press the green button Write a new story.

  2. Type the title

    For example: Rhinos of Kaziranga – Day 1.

  3. Choose the country and the trip

    Pick the country from the list. Then pick the trip, for example Ranthambore. For a new place, choose + Start a new trip… and type its name. If the story is about animals, choose Yes so it also appears on the Wild Life Safaris page.

  4. Write the story

    Type in the white box. Press Enter to start a new paragraph. You can also copy text from Word or an email and paste it in.

  5. Add photos

    Click in the text where the photos should go. Press Add photos, choose one or more photos, and wait a moment while they upload. Under each photo you can type a caption. Use Move up and Move down to change the order.

  6. Check it, then publish

    Press Preview to see exactly how readers will see it. When you are happy, press Publish to website.

Good to know

  • Nothing is lost. Drafts save themselves every half minute.
  • Not finished? Press Save as draft. Readers cannot see drafts. Open it again later from My stories.
  • Fixing a mistake in a published story: open it, change it, press Save changes.
  • To take a story off the website, open it and press Hide from website. You can publish it again any time.
  • If something looks wrong, simply call for help. Nothing you do here can break the website’s design.
